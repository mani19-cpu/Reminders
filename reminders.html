Add to Your Phone

To install Reminders on your phone, first host the files online (e.g. GitHub Pages), then open the link in your mobile browser.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)
  1. Open the site in Safari
  2. Tap the Share button ⎋
  3. Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap Add
Android (Chrome)
  1. Open the site in Chrome
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu
  3. Tap "Add to Home Screen"
  4. Tap Add
💡 Quick host option: Upload reminders.html, manifest.json, icon.svg and sw.js to GitHub Pages for a free permanent link.
